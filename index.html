📖 Quick guide

How a hand works

Two forced bets (small blind & big blind) start the pot. Everyone gets 2 private cards, then up to four betting rounds: preflop → flop (3 shared cards) → turn (1 more) → river (1 more). Best 5-card hand from your 2 + 5 shared cards wins. You can also win by making everyone fold.

Hand rankings (best → worst)

  1. Royal / Straight Flush — five in a row, same suit
  2. Four of a Kind — e.g. 9 9 9 9
  3. Full House — three of a kind + a pair
  4. Flush — five of the same suit
  5. Straight — five in a row (A can be high or low)
  6. Three of a Kind
  7. Two Pair
  8. One Pair
  9. High Card

Position is power

The dealer button (BTN) acts last after the flop — you see what everyone does before deciding. That's why you can play more hands from the button/cutoff (CO) and should play very tight from under the gun (UTG) and the blinds.

Pot odds in 20 seconds

If you must call $20 into an $80 pot, you're paying 20 to win 100 → you need to win 20% of the time to break even. Rule of thumb for draws: outs × 4 ≈ your % on the flop (two cards to come), outs × 2 on the turn.

Know your opponents

  • 🪨 Rocky (tight & careful) — barely plays a hand. When he bets big, fold unless you're strong. Steal his blinds.
  • 🦈 Tanya (tight-aggressive) — the dangerous one. Solid hands, played hard. Avoid marginal spots against her.
  • 🃏 Loco (loose & wild) — plays anything, bluffs often. Call down lighter and let him hang himself.
  • 📞 Callie (calling station) — never folds a pair. Value bet heavily, never bluff.
  • 🎩 Prof (solid all-round) — balanced play, respects aggression.
